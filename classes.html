PHYS 512 · Quantum Field Theory I

In progress Instructor: [name] 4 credits

Canonical quantization of free fields, the Feynman propagator, and perturbation theory up to one loop. The organising object is the generating functional $Z[J] = \int \mathcal{D}\phi \; e^{i S[\phi] + i\int J\phi}$, from which every correlation function falls out by differentiation.

MATH 405 · Real Analysis

In progress Instructor: [name] 3 credits

Measure theory, the Lebesgue integral, and $L^p$ spaces. The result everything else leans on: if $f_n \to f$ pointwise and $\abs{f_n} \le g$ with $g$ integrable, then $\lim_n \int f_n = \int f$ — dominated convergence.

PHYS 431 · Statistical Mechanics

Completed Instructor: [name] 4 credits

Ensembles, partition functions, and phase transitions. Everything follows from $Z = \sum_i e^{-\beta E_i}$ and the free energy $F = -k_B T \ln Z$.

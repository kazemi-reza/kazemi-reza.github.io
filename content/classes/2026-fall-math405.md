Measure theory, the Lebesgue integral, and $L^p$ spaces. The result everything
else leans on: if $f_n \to f$ pointwise and $\abs{f_n} \le g$ with $g$
integrable, then $\lim_n \int f_n = \int f$ — dominated convergence.

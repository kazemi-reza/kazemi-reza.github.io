Canonical quantization of free fields, the Feynman propagator, and perturbation
theory up to one loop. The organising object is the generating functional
$Z[J] = \int \mathcal{D}\phi \; e^{i S[\phi] + i\int J\phi}$, from which every
correlation function falls out by differentiation.
